Zombie Survival Game

Rules are explained in the youth room at 6:50.

  1. Band on your ARM = human. Band on your HEAD = zombie.
  2. Zombies tag with two open hands, below the neck. If you're tagged: count to 20 out loud, move your band to your head. You're a zombie now.
  3. Hit a zombie with a dart or sock ball and they kneel and count to 30. Then they're back up.
  4. Zombies can't hold blasters, can't hold ammo, can't touch ammo.
  5. If you're carrying a mission item you can't shoot. Get tagged, drop it where you stand.
  6. Home Base is safe. Nobody tags, nobody shoots, inside Home Base.
  7. Complete the objective.
Mission 1

Power

Six fuse cards are scattered around the play zone. Get four of them to the generator station. When the fourth lands: air horn, and a leader flips the gym and hallway lights on.

Easy • 5-10 mins
Mission 2

Collect Vials

Bring 25 vials to home base in order to research an antidote (large glow sticks). A vial can be used to either heal a zombie or research an antidote. If a human is tagged with a vial they can choose to heal the zombie, or become infected (dropping the vial where they were tagged).

Medium • 10-15 mins
Mission 3

Heal the Infected

Using the antidote that was created, humans must hunt and shoot all the infected zombies with the antidote to heal them and turn them back into humans before the time is up.

Hard • 10-15 mins
